Privacy Policy

Fletchers Cobblers Ltd · till invoicing connection to QuickBooks Online · last updated 7 October 2026

This policy covers the private software Fletchers Cobblers Ltd uses to connect its shop tills to its own QuickBooks Online company, so that work done for account customers can be invoiced. It is used only by Fletchers Cobblers Ltd and the shops it invoices for. It is not offered to the public or to other QuickBooks users.

Who is responsible

Fletchers Cobblers Ltd is the data controller for the information described here.

What information is used

  • Account customers from QuickBooks: each customer's display name, QuickBooks reference number and whether they are active. This list is copied so shop staff can pick the right customer on the till.
  • Work charged to account customers: the date and time, a short description written by shop staff (for example who collected the work or which property it was for), the price, and which shop took it.
  • Invoice records: the QuickBooks invoice reference and totals for each monthly invoice, plus a log of each monthly run.
  • The QuickBooks connection: the access tokens QuickBooks issues to this connection, and the company reference they belong to.

Card details, bank details and passwords are never collected or stored by this software.

How it is used

  • To show the account customer list on the shop tills.
  • To create one invoice per customer each month in Fletchers Cobblers Ltd's QuickBooks Online company, listing the work done.
  • To ask QuickBooks to email each invoice to the customer at the email address held in QuickBooks.

The software does not edit or delete existing QuickBooks records, and shop staff are never given access to QuickBooks.

Where it is kept

Information is held by Intuit in QuickBooks Online, and in a private database hosted by Supabase in the United Kingdom (London region). The QuickBooks connection tokens are kept only on the server, in a part of the database that the shop tills and the public cannot read. Access is limited to the people who run Fletchers Cobblers Ltd's accounts.

Sharing

Information is not sold, rented or shared for marketing. It is only processed by Intuit (QuickBooks Online) and Supabase (hosting) to provide the service described above, under their own terms and privacy policies.

How long it is kept

Charge and invoice records are kept for as long as needed for Fletchers Cobblers Ltd's accounts, normally six years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK law requires for business records.

Disconnecting

The connection can be removed at any time from the Apps section of QuickBooks Online. Once disconnected, the software can no longer read from or write to QuickBooks.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or object to how it is used. Please contact Fletchers Cobblers Ltd through any of its shops. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
